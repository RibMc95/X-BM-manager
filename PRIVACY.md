# Privacy Policy — X Bookmark Manager

_Last updated: 2026-06-26_

X Bookmark Manager stores the tweet links you choose to save, along with the
category you assign them, **locally on your own device** using the browser's
`chrome.storage.local` API.

- **We do not collect, transmit, or sell any data.** Nothing leaves your browser.
- **No remote servers.** The extension makes no network requests of its own.
- **No tracking or analytics.** We do not use cookies, identifiers, or third-party services.
- **Your data stays with you.** Saved links live only in your browser's local
  storage. Exporting produces a JSON file on your device; importing reads one
  you provide. You can remove all data at any time by removing your saved items
  or uninstalling the extension.
