Privacy Policy

X Bookmark Manager (Firefox add-on) · Last updated: June 28, 2026

X Bookmark Manager is a Firefox add-on that lets you save links to tweets and organize them into your own categories. This policy explains how it handles your data. In short: the add-on collects no data, and everything stays on your own device.

Data collection

This add-on does not collect, transmit, or share any data. It declares data_collection_permissions: { required: ["none"] } in its manifest, consistent with Mozilla's data-consent requirements.

What the add-on stores locally

When you save a tweet, the following is stored only on your own device using Firefox's storage.local API:

  • The tweet's link (URL) that you chose to save
  • The category you assign to it
  • The date and time you saved it

This data never leaves your browser and is never sent to the developer or any third party.

What the add-on does not do

  • No servers or network requests. The add-on makes no outbound connections of its own.
  • No tracking. No analytics, cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party services.
  • No selling or sharing. Because nothing is collected, nothing is ever sold, transferred, or shared.

Permissions

  • activeTab — reads the current tab's URL only when you click "Save current tab," so it can be saved.
  • storage — saves your bookmark list locally on your device.
  • contextMenus — adds the right-click "Save to X Bookmark Manager" menu on x.com and twitter.com.

Your control over your data

You can remove any saved item at any time from the add-on's popup, or export your full list to a JSON file for your own backup. Removing the add-on deletes all of its stored data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will be reflected on the add-on's listing.
