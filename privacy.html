Privacy Policy

X Bookmark Manager · Last updated: June 27, 2026

X Bookmark Manager lets you save links to tweets and organize them into your own categories. This policy explains what the extension does with your data. In short: everything stays on your device, and nothing is ever sent to us or anyone else.

What the extension stores

When you save a tweet, the extension stores the following locally on your own device using the browser's chrome.storage.local API:

  • The tweet's link (URL) that you chose to save
  • The category you assign to it
  • The date and time you saved it

What we do not do

  • No data collection. We do not collect, receive, or have any access to your saved links or categories.
  • No servers. The extension makes no network requests of its own. Your data never leaves your browser.
  • No tracking. No analytics, cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party services are used.
  • No selling or sharing. Because nothing is collected, nothing is ever sold, transferred, or shared.

Permissions

  • activeTab — reads the current tab's URL only when you click "Save current tab," so it can be saved.
  • storage — saves your bookmark list locally on your device.
  • contextMenus — adds the right-click "Save to X Bookmark Manager" menu on x.com and twitter.com.

Your control over your data

You can remove any saved item at any time from the extension's popup, or export your full list to a JSON file for your own backup. Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will be reflected in the extension's store listing.
